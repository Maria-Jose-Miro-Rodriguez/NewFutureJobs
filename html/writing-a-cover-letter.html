Writing: A cover Letter

HOW TO WRITE A COVER LETTER

A Cover Letter (or covering letter) is a document sent with your CV to introduce yourself and explain why you are the best candidate for a job. At your level, focus on clear, formal communication .

Structure:

Greeting: Dear Hiring Manager / Dear Mr./Ms. [Surname].
Opening: State the position you are applying for.
Body: Highlight 2-3 skills and your motivation [4].
Closing: Express interest in an interview.
Sign-off: Yours sincerely (if you know the name) or Yours faithfully.
Example:

Dear Ms. Smith,

I am writing to apply for the Receptionist position. I have a B1 level of English and two years of experience in customer service. I am hardworking and enjoy working in teams.

I would like to have the opportunity of having a personal interview.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,

[Your Name].

 
Exercises:

Fill in the blanks: Complete the sentence: "I am writing to _______ for the position of Sales Assistant because I have relevant _______."

Writing: Write a short (50-80 words) cover letter for a "Summer Camp Monitor" job using the structure above
