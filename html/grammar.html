FUTURE FORMS IN ENGLISH

Look at the different types of future forms in English. 

Practice Exercises
Exercise  1. Choose the correct option (Multiple Choice)

Look at those clouds! It (will / is going to) rain.
I (am flying / fly) to London tomorrow; I have the tickets!
The film (starts / is starting) at 9:00 PM tonight.
I'm tired. I think I (will go / am going to go) to sleep.
In 2050, people (will be able to / are able to) live on Mars.
To get the job, you (will have to / will able to) speak English.

Exercise 2. Fill in the blanks (Mixed Futures)

I _________ (travel) to Japan next summer. (Plan)
Wait! I _________ (help) you with those bags. (Decision)
The bus _________ (arrive) at 10:15 AM. (Timetable)
We _________ (have) dinner with Ben tonight. (Arrangement)
You _________ (need) a passport for the trip. (Obligation)
Humans _________ (not / be able to) breathe underwater. (Ability)


Exercise 3. Sentence Transformation (Rewrite)

It is my intention to learn French. (Use going to)
The plane departure is at 5:00 PM. (Use Present Simple)
I promise to call you later. (Use will)
It’s necessary for him to work tomorrow. (Use will have to)
A meeting with the doctor is set for Monday. (Use Present Continuous)
Is it possible for her to drive next year? (Use will be able to)
