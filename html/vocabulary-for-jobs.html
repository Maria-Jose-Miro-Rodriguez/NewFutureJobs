Vocabulary for jobs

JOB VOCABULARY

Job definitions

Colleague # The person you work with

Deadline # The final date or time to complete a task

Manager # The person who leads a team or department

Salary # The money a worker receives for their job

Meeting # A planned gathering where people discuss work

Promotion # A move to a higher position at work

Shift # The period of time a person works during the day or night

Interview # A formal conversation to evaluate a candidate for a job

Coworker # A person who works at the same company as you

Resignation # The act of leaving a job officially
