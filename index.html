Working for the future:  Are You Ready?

The job market is changing rapidly due to technology and globalization. In the coming years, many traditional roles might disappear, while new ones related to Artificial Intelligence and sustainability will emerge. One of the biggest challenges you will face is "adaptability." It is no longer just about what you know, but how quickly you can learn new skills in an inclusive and digital environment.

Throughout this unit, we will explore these global challenges and prepare ourselves for an exciting professional future. To succeed, you need to master specific tools that showcase your talent. First, we will learn how to write a professional cover letter and a basic text to introduce yourselves. We will also build personal portfolios to highlight your achievements. Finally, we will practice mock interviews using AI simulators and oral techniques to improve your confidence . By the end of this module, you will have the essential skills to navigate the modern working world with success. Let’s start this journey together!
